    Practise with your own sheet music

    1. Open a MusicXML score (.musicxml, .xml or .mxl). MuseScore and most notation apps can export one.
    2. Play it back slowly, loop a few measures, and play along.
    3. Record yourself, or open a recording, to see every note colored by how in tune and on time it was.

    Everything stays on this device. Nothing is uploaded.

                    How converting works

                    Reading a PDF into MusicXML (with homr) needs your PC's graphics card, so it runs there, not on the phone. This screen leaves a small note in the library's requests/ folder for each PDF you queue.

                    1. On your PC, the watcher converts queued PDFs by itself whenever the PC is on. Set it up once, in the library folder: lento-library watch --install. Without it, run lento-library convert by hand (or lento-library convert --missing for every PDF without MusicXML).
                    2. It saves each MusicXML beside its PDF with the same name, then rebuilds library.json.
                    3. Press Refresh here or in the Library, and the new scores are ready to practise.

                    Fix clef, key, time or instrument

                      Each change runs from that measure to the next clef, key or time change in the score. Approve saves them; your PC then updates the MusicXML.

                        Google Drive library

                        Press Connect and sign in: the app finds your library (the folder with library.json) by itself. To use a different folder, paste its link first.

                        Advanced

                        The app reads your library and only ever creates its own small request files in requests/. Scores you open are saved on this device so they work offline.

                        Tuning

                        Used by the tuner, playback and the intonation feedback.

                        Headphone delay

                        off

                        Wireless headphones play sound late. This holds the moving cursor back to match. Practice settings has a tap test that measures it.

                        How strictly notes are judged

                        Your own windows, for when the presets in Practice don't fit. Changing one here switches Practice's Pitch or Timing menu to Custom; the presets stay there to switch back.

                        Further out than "slightly off" is out of tune, and past 70 cents a note counts as a wrong note. Real bowing is rarely closer than about 60 ms to the beat, so very tight timing flags most notes.

                        Instrument sounds

                        Playback always starts at once with the built-in synth. Recordings of each instrument in the score download in the background (a few hundred KB each), take over when ready, and stay on this device for offline use. Sounds: VS Chamber Orchestra Community Edition (CC0).
