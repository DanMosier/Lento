Practise with your own sheet music

  1. Open a MusicXML score (.musicxml, .xml or .mxl). MuseScore and most notation apps can export one.
  2. Play it back slowly, loop a few measures, and play along.
  3. Record yourself, or open a recording, to see every note colored by how in tune and on time it was.

Everything stays on this device. Nothing is uploaded.

    Library settings

    The app only reads your Drive. Scores you open are saved on this device so they work offline.
